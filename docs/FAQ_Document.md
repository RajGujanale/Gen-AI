## Admissions

### Q1. What are the eligibility criteria for admission to the B.Tech (CSE) programme?
**Answer:** To be eligible for the B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering programme, a candidate must have passed 10+2 (or an equivalent examination) with Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry or Computer Science, securing an aggregate of 50% marks (45% for candidates belonging to reserved categories as per state government norms). Admission is offered against the rank secured in the Riverdale Common Entrance Test (RCET), or through the management quota as per college rules.

### Q2. How do I apply for admission to the department?
**Answer:** Applications are submitted online. Register on the college admissions portal, complete the application form, upload the required documents in the prescribed format, pay the non-refundable application fee of INR 500 through the available digital payment options, and submit the form. Print the confirmation page and carry it to the counselling venue on the notified date.

### Q3. What is the last date to apply for admission?
**Answer:** For the 2026-27 academic session, the last date to submit the online application is 30 June 2026. The first merit list is published on 5 July 2026, and counselling for merit-listed candidates is held from 10 to 12 July 2026. Applications received after these dates are not considered.

### Q4. Which documents are required at the time of admission?
**Answer:** Candidates must produce the 10th and 12th standard mark sheets and passing certificates, the transfer certificate and migration certificate from the last institution studied, the RCET score card (or the score card of an equivalent entrance test), caste and income certificates if applicable, four recent passport-size photographs, and a copy of the Aadhaar card. Originals are verified and returned; self-attested copies are retained by the department.

### Q5. What are the fees for the programme?
**Answer:** The tuition fee for the B.Tech (CSE) programme is INR 85,000 per academic year. A one-time admission fee of INR 10,000 is payable at the time of admission, and a refundable caution deposit of INR 5,000 is collected once. Fees may be paid in two instalments, one at the beginning of each semester, through the college fee portal.

### Q6. Are scholarships available for students?
**Answer:** Yes. Merit scholarships covering 25% of the tuition fee are awarded to the top 10% of admitted students based on their entrance test rank. Students from reserved categories may apply for state government post-matric scholarships through the e-pass portal, and a sports quota concession is available to students who have represented their state at national-level events, subject to verification of certificates.

### Q7. How many seats are available in the department?
**Answer:** The department offers an intake of 120 seats in B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering, 60 seats in B.Tech Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, and 18 seats in M.Tech Computer Science and Engineering. Seats are filled on the basis of RCET rank in the order of merit, and the seat matrix is displayed on the admissions portal.

## Courses

### Q8. Which courses are offered by the department?
**Answer:** The department offers four programmes: B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering, B.Tech Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, M.Tech Computer Science and Engineering, and a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) programme in Computer Science and Engineering. The first two are undergraduate programmes, M.Tech is a postgraduate programme, and the PhD is a research programme.

### Q9. What is the duration of each course?
**Answer:** The B.Tech programmes in Computer Science and Engineering and in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science are four years long and are completed over eight semesters. The M.Tech Computer Science and Engineering programme is two years long over four semesters, and the PhD programme has a minimum duration of three years.

### Q10. How is the syllabus organised?
**Answer:** The syllabus is revised every three years and is delivered semester-wise. Semesters 1 and 2 provide a common foundation in mathematics, physics, chemistry, basic electronics and introductory programming. Semesters 3 to 6 carry the core computer science subjects, while semesters 7 and 8 carry department electives, the capstone project and the internship.

### Q11. What are the main subjects taught in the programme?
**Answer:** The core subjects of the B.Tech (CSE) programme include C programming, data structures and algorithms, object-oriented programming, database management systems, operating systems, computer networks, software engineering, theory of computation, compiler design, web technologies, artificial intelligence and machine learning. Mathematics, communication skills and professional ethics are also taught across the semesters.

### Q12. How does the credit system work?
**Answer:** Each course carries a fixed number of credits corresponding to the number of lecture hours per week, and laboratory courses carry credits based on contact hours. A B.Tech student must earn about 168 credits to graduate, and semester performance is graded on a 10-point CGPA scale as per the regulations of the awarding university.

### Q13. What electives are available to students?
**Answer:** From the seventh semester, students may choose department electives such as cloud computing, cyber security, data science, internet of things and full-stack development. Students may also take open electives offered by other departments and MOOC-based electives through the college credit-transfer scheme, subject to availability of seats.

### Q14. What is the attendance requirement?
**Answer:** A student must secure a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject and in aggregate to be permitted to write the end-semester examination. Shortages arising from medical grounds may be condoned by the department on the recommendation of the class adviser, with supporting medical certificates.

### Q15. Are practical sessions part of the curriculum?
**Answer:** Every core theory course is supported by a corresponding laboratory session of three to four contact hours per week. Laboratory work is assessed through internal record work, experiments conducted in the laboratory, and a viva-voce examination at the end of the semester.

## Examinations

### Q16. When are the examinations conducted?
**Answer:** Internal unit tests are conducted in September-October and in March-April of each academic year, and the end-semester examinations are conducted in November-December and in May-June. The detailed datesheet is published on the student portal and on the department notice board at least 15 days before the examination begins.

### Q17. How can I apply for the semester examination?
**Answer:** Examination applications are submitted through the student portal. Log in with the enrolment number, open the Examination tab, select the current semester, verify the subject codes shown, pay the examination fee online, and submit the form before the notified last date. Applications submitted after the last date require a late fee of INR 200 and the approval of the examination in-charge.

### Q18. How are internal assessments calculated?
**Answer:** Each subject carries 100 marks, of which 30 marks are internal and 70 marks are external. The internal component is calculated as the average of the two unit tests (20 marks) together with assignments, attendance and laboratory performance (10 marks), as per the regulations of the awarding university.

### Q19. How do I obtain my hall ticket?
**Answer:** The hall ticket can be downloaded from the student portal three working days before the examination. It is generated only for candidates who have the required attendance and have cleared the semester fee. Students must carry the printed hall ticket together with the college identity card to every examination hall.

### Q20. What are the passing criteria?
**Answer:** A student must secure at least 40% of the total marks in each subject, taking the internal and external components together, and must secure 40% in the aggregate of the semester in order to pass. A pass grade is awarded in each subject separately and is carried forward to the cumulative grade sheet.

### Q21. How can I apply for revaluation of my answer script?
**Answer:** Revaluation can be requested within seven days of the declaration of the result by submitting an online request through the student portal with a fee of INR 300 per subject. The revised result is communicated within 15 working days, and the revised marks are updated in the grade sheet.

### Q22. Are supplementary examinations conducted?
**Answer:** Yes. Supplementary examinations for the subjects in which a student has failed are conducted in September and in March-April of every year. Eligible students must apply through the student portal by paying INR 500 per subject before the notified last date.

### Q23. Where and when are the results published?
**Answer:** Results of the internal tests and the end-semester examinations are published on the student portal within four to six weeks of the last working day of the examination. The provisional grade card can be downloaded from the portal, and the signed consolidated mark statement is issued by the examination section.

## Placements

### Q24. What is the eligibility to appear for placements?
**Answer:** To be eligible for campus placements, a student must have an aggregate of 60% or above from the first semester up to the pre-final semester, must have no active backlogs at the time of the recruitment drive, must have a minimum of 75% attendance, and must have completed all pre-placement training modules.

### Q25. How does campus recruitment work at the college?
**Answer:** The Training and Placement Cell coordinates all campus recruitment activities. Company drives are conducted mainly between October and March for final-year students, and internship drives are conducted for pre-final-year students. Companies register with the cell, shortlist students from the eligible list, and conduct their selection process on campus.

### Q26. Are internship opportunities provided to students?
**Answer:** Yes. Pre-final-year students undertake a summer internship of six to eight weeks during the vacation, which carries credit in the eighth semester. The Training and Placement Cell shares internship notifications, and students may also apply on their own subject to approval by the department through the internship consent form.

### Q27. Does the college provide placement training?
**Answer:** Yes. Pre-placement training begins in the fifth semester and covers aptitude and reasoning, communication and group discussion skills, resume writing, and technical preparation in data structures, DBMS, operating systems and programming. The training includes mock interviews, guest sessions from recruiters, and a resume-building workshop conducted by the Training and Placement Cell.

### Q28. Which companies visit the campus for recruitment?
**Answer:** Recruiters that have visited the campus include Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Cognizant, Accenture, Capgemini and LTIMindtree, along with product companies such as TechNova Solutions and DataCraft Analytics. The list of visiting companies for the current season is published on the Training and Placement Cell notice board.

### Q29. How do I prepare my resume for placements?
**Answer:** Students are expected to prepare a one-page resume in a standard format. The Training and Placement Cell conducts a resume-writing workshop in the sixth semester and offers one-to-one resume review sessions; the reviewed resume must be approved by the placement coordinator before it is shared with recruiters.

### Q30. What is the interview procedure during campus placements?
**Answer:** The selection process generally consists of four rounds: an online aptitude and coding test, two technical interviews, and a final HR interview. Students must register their consent through the Training and Placement Cell before each drive, report to the venue at the notified time with two printed copies of the resume and the college identity card, and follow the dress code communicated by the recruiter.
